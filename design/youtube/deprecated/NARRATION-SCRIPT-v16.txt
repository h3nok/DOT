DOT — FILM NARRATION SCRIPT (v16, the whole film) · FOR THE AUTHOR TO READ
====================================================================

Read all 70 lines in one recording, in order: the opening, then each concept's name and definition,
the interlude, and the closing. The film places each line as it appears on screen.
A calm, unhurried voice. Where a line ends with a pause in the film, let it land.

HOW TO RECORD
- A quiet room with soft furnishings. A phone voice memo is fine, held 20–30 cm from your mouth.
- Read each line once, calmly, a little slower than conversation.
- Leave about two seconds of silence between lines (count "one, two" in your head). The pauses
  are how the recording is split into lines, so keep them silent and keep them longer than
  any pause inside a line.
- Do not read the [DOT proposes] labels; they appear on screen.
- Read "RF₀" as you say it in conversation (for example "R F zero"). Little c is "little see".
- If you stumble, pause, read the whole line again, and tell me its number.
- For a concept, say its name, then its definition, as one sentence pair.

WHEN YOU ARE DONE
Save the file (m4a or wav) to artifacts/voiceover/film/ and tell me its name. Then:
  pnpm dlx vite-node scripts/film-voiceover.mjs --recording=<file>
splits, trims and masters it, and the film is exported with:
  node scripts/render-dark-film.mjs --voiceover=<the run directory it prints>
