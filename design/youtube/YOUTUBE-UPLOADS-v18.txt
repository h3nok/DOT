1. TRAILER (suggested as the channel trailer)
---------------------------------------------
TITLE
Digital Organism Theory: An Introduction

DESCRIPTION
We go to work. We pay bills. We scroll. We compete. We call exhaustion adulthood and anxiety
responsibility. This film asks what we call progress, and whether the person is developing along
with the machinery. It asks what the establishment, the scientific community included, owes for its
part, and why a culture that teaches everyone they can be anything leaves no moral foundation. It
follows one person through a culture that arrives as ordinary life, and an arrangement that can
profit from keeping people unsettled.

Digital Organism Theory (DOT), by Henok Ghebrechristos, is a school of thought for examining
experience and expanding awareness. It begins from experience itself: feeling treated as data, but
not automatically as truth. It is offered as a construction to examine, not a revelation to believe.

DOT's own claims are labelled "DOT proposes" on screen. They are proposals, not established
science. Book One sets out the argument, its rival explanations, and what would count against it.
The moral-erosion scene uses an illustration from The American Millennial Manifesto: AI-generated,
directed by the author, and labelled so on screen.

TITLE
Book One, concept by concept — Digital Organism Theory

DESCRIPTION
The concerns behind Digital Organism Theory, a careful introduction to it, then all seventeen
concepts of Book One in the order of the Concept Map. Each concept has its own drawing, its
definition, an everyday analogy, and its claim level: Observation, Model, Hypothesis, or
Speculation. Those labels show how much support each idea has, and which are still untested.

The book is free to read. Captions are available.
