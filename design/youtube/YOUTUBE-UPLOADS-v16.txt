1. TRAILER (suggested as the channel trailer)
---------------------------------------------
TITLE
Digital Organism Theory: An Introduction

DESCRIPTION
We go to work. We pay bills. We scroll. We compete. We call exhaustion adulthood and anxiety
responsibility. Marketing fills every space we look at, screens compete for every minute of our
attention, and money speaks. This film follows one person through that culture and what it
costs: confusion, a body under strain, and success that never fills the gap.

We are not immune to our culture or our surroundings. None of that is our fault. Undoing it is
our responsibility.

Digital Organism Theory (DOT) is a school of thought for examining experience, developed by Henok
Ghebrechristos after a career in software and AI. From the film: "I don't claim
to know everything. But I feel the erosion. I see the despair." DOT aims to be a foundation for
a cultural change.

DOT's own claims are labelled "DOT proposes" on screen. They are proposals, not established
science. Book One sets out the argument, its rival explanations, and what would count against it.

2. FULL FILM

TITLE
Book One, concept by concept — Digital Organism Theory

DESCRIPTION
An introduction in Henok Ghebrechristos's own words, then all seventeen concepts of Digital
Organism Theory, Book One, in the order of the Concept Map. Each concept has its own drawing,
its definition, an everyday analogy, and its claim level: Observation, Model, Hypothesis, or
Speculation. Those labels show how much support each idea has, and which are still untested.

The book is free to read. Captions are available.
