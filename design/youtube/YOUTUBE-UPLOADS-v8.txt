TITLE
Digital Organism Theory — A pragmatic, testable framework for experience

DESCRIPTION
Every observation begins as an experience. This short film introduces Digital Organism
Theory (DOT), a framework developed by Henok Ghebrechristos from his own experience
and offered for each viewer to test against theirs.

It moves from what is widely accepted — perception is formed from sensory data — to
what DOT proposes: that you are a Little c, an experiencer, and that the body is your
interface. It sets that proposal beside a widely held view, quoted from Francis Crick's
The Astonishing Hypothesis (1994), and follows what happens when experience is left
out of the account: a gap that markets, platforms, and ideologies fill, and stress
that is treated more than its source.

Claim levels: perception and stress lines draw on mainstream science (External model);
"You are Little c" and the architecture are DOT's proposals (DOT derivation /
Speculative extension). Nothing here asks for belief — only a test against your own
experience.

TITLE
Book One, concept by concept — Digital Organism Theory

DESCRIPTION
The opening film, followed by each of the seventeen concepts in Book One, Consciousness:
A Digital Organism, in the order of the Concept Map — each with its own picture, the
Concept Map's definition, and an everyday analogy. The words are typed on screen; there
is no narration, and the film plays only as fast as it can be read.

Every concept carries its claim level on screen: Observation, External model, DOT
derivation, or Speculative extension. DOT is presented as a set of proposals to be tested
in each viewer's own experience, not as settled fact.
