# ADR-0042: Love and honest inquiry at the front door

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Deciders:** Founder
- **Amends:** ADR-0030 and ADR-0038 (homepage opening and final action only)

## Context

The founder asked for an honest homepage and described Love as a state in which
Fear ceases to govern, developed through years of reflection, honesty, and
accountability. He distinguishes his experience from the explanations he infers
from it, and says those explanations remain open to revision. His invitation is
to read, inquire, ask, and change, at the reader's own pace. He also asked for Love
to be expressed through color.

The released Book One preface already gives the relevant definition and claim
boundaries. The homepage's emphasis on architecture and a growing intellectual
movement obscured that human purpose; its final primary action led to the Academy
rather than the book.

## Decision

Open with Love and an attributed quotation from the released preface: “Love is
the condition in which Fear no longer governs you.” Make reading Book One the
first action, before the concept introduction, and the primary final action.
The reading invitation acknowledges that change can be long and difficult,
allows the reader to take their time, and identifies explanations of a larger
consciousness and nonphysical substrate as hypotheses.

Replace the unsupported movement-status headline with an invitation to inquire.
Keep the Academy as a distinct, secondary route, and retain the non-endorsement
boundary around related thinkers. Keep the outside-in architecture sequence,
source links, and the finite, reader-controlled concept introduction authorized
by ADR-0037.

Use a static spread of the reader's selected accent around Love. It adds no
animation, does not recolor text, and disappears in high contrast, field-off,
and print modes. Reuse the same editorial content in the runtime homepage and
its script-free HTML. The released manuscript is unchanged; quotations are
attributed, and new descriptions do not impersonate the author's voice.

## Consequences

The first encounter gives the human purpose before the theoretical vocabulary.
Reading remains free and immediate. This serves L1 (predictable and calm),
L2 (natural completion), L6 (no manufactured loss or deadline), L7 (honest claim
levels), L10 (one primary reading action), and L12 (serving the reader's declared
intention). No manifesto law is
violated; no tracking, urgency, counters, or new interruptive behavior is added.

The homepage no longer claims movement status. The Academy remains available
for developing definitions and questions; Book One stays a fixed edition.
Future experience or evidence may revise the framing without rewriting the
released book.

## Amendment — 2026-10-10

At the founder’s request, the homepage now opens with “Love is the answer.”
and the primary reading action is “Read Book One.” The opening has no supporting paragraph or action subtitle.
Critiques of mainstream science, physicalism, and secular humanism appear
within the concept introduction, labeled “Outside perspective” or “DOT rejects” at Model
level. Primary sources are linked where available; references support the
underlying position or problem, not an endorsement of DOT. The criticism
targets exclusion in advance; it does not claim that all humanists or all
scientific research exclude subjective experience. Love
is defined within the theory as the condition in which Fear no longer governs;
the opening states the founder’s proposition, not a settled scientific result. The released manuscript is unchanged.

Remove the “Things built. Ideas explored.” section and its three cards from the
homepage, generated HTML, and page map. Work, writing, and contact remain available
through the site navigation. This amendment serves L7 (clear and honest framing)
and L10 (a focused reading invitation) without violating any manifesto law.

## Opening refinement — 2026-10-10

The founder’s current heading is “Trust your experience.” The requested two-line
subtitle treats feelings as data and invites readers to “Tune your interpretation
toward coherence.” Tuning here means development toward a coherent, low-entropy
attitude in DOT’s vocabulary, including less inner conflict. Only this tuning
clause takes bold copper; the invitation to challenge models that exclude the
reader or ask them to be someone else uses neutral body text. There is one
emphasis rather than competing colored statements. High contrast preserves
that emphasis with an underline. This is an
editorial invitation to inquiry, not a claim that interoception research proves
DOT’s proposed architecture. Research distinguishes sensing bodily signals
from interpreting them ([Khalsa et al., 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6054486/)).
The runtime and script-free homepage carry the same wording and emphasis. This
serves L7’s honest framing and L10’s concise reading invitation without violating
any manifesto law or changing the book.
