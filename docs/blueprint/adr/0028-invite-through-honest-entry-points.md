# ADR-0028: Invite Through Honest Entry Points

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Deciders:** Founder
- **Amends:** ADR-0022 (one reading path beneath the primary action)

## Context

The theory-first landing established conceptual clarity, but clarity alone did
not tell a reader why the inquiry might matter to them. Readers may arrive
seeking intellectual renewal, spiritual grounding, language for lived
experience, or a serious place to challenge the theory. Presenting only the
written-order argument makes those readers translate their own need into the
author's structure before they can begin.

The invitation must remain compatible with ADR-0022: it cannot pressure a reader
to join, fund, or profess belief. It also cannot offer spiritual meaning by
lowering the theory's evidentiary standards.

## Decision

The public entry will address the reader directly:

- DOT can be examined, challenged, used in part, or rejected.
- Spiritual meaning and intellectual rigor are presented as compatible needs,
  not rival loyalties.
- The evidence boundary separates publicly grounded observations from DOT's
  still-proposed ontology before inviting further reading.
- Three quiet entry paths follow the core theory: start with the architecture,
  start where you live, or start with the objections.

These are finite text links, not engagement tiles or personalized routing. “Read
Book One” remains the single primary action. Join and funding asks remain away
from the front door.

## Consequences

**Positive.** Readers can recognize their reason for arriving and choose a
proportionate beginning. The evidence boundary becomes an invitation grounded
in trust rather than a defensive disclaimer. The page offers spiritual anchoring
without asking readers to surrender judgment.

**Negative.** The landing contains more than one secondary route, and some copy
repeats the book's epistemic commitments in direct reader language.

**Revisit if** the paths become promotional, expand into a recommendation
engine, or reader research shows that three starting points create hesitation
rather than agency.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
| ------ | ---- | ---- | ------- |
| Keep only the written-order book route | Maximum simplicity | Makes every reader begin from the author's structure | Rejected |
| Lead with spiritual promises | Emotionally immediate | Overclaims outcomes and weakens epistemic trust | Rejected |
| Offer three honest, finite starting points | Preserves agency; names real reader needs | Adds secondary choices | **Chosen** |
