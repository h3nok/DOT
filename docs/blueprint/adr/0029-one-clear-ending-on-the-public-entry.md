# ADR-0029: One Clear Ending on the Public Entry

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Deciders:** Founder
- **Supersedes:** ADR-0028 (three entry paths on the landing page)
- **Amends:** ADR-0026 (ten-term vocabulary ledger on the landing page)

## Context

The theory-led entry already introduces the architecture in the hero and then
explains T · E, Big C, RF₀, and Little c in four full teaching panels. A later
orientation section repeated the invitation in three paths and displayed all
ten vocabulary terms before the reader reached the final Book One action.

That material was accurate, but it created a second table of contents after the
theory had already been taught. The added choices weakened the transition from
the model's evidence boundary into the book itself.

## Decision

After the four theory panels, the public entry will move directly through:

1. one explicit evidence boundary, including what is publicly grounded and
   what DOT still proposes; and
2. one final invitation to read Book One, with the definitions surface kept as
   a quiet secondary reference.

The separate “Intellectual renewal · Spiritual grounding” section, its three
entry paths, and the ten-concept vocabulary ledger are removed from the landing
page. The concepts and alternate reading routes may remain available on focused
book or reference surfaces, where readers are actively seeking that depth.

Motion in the remaining sequence is finite, restrained, and disabled when the
reader or the site's stillness control requests reduced motion.

## Consequences

**Positive.** The landing has one conceptual sequence and one ending. Readers
encounter less repetition and fewer choices before the primary action. The
evidence boundary carries more weight, and the page better serves L1 and L10.

**Negative.** Readers can no longer select a theme-specific starting point from
the landing page, and the ten terms are less immediately scannable there.

**Revisit if** reader testing shows that people understand the four-layer model
but cannot find the definitions or a suitable place to begin Book One.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
| ------ | ---- | ---- | ------- |
| Keep three entry paths and the full vocabulary ledger | Maximum choice and immediate reference | Repeats the page's structure and delays the primary action | Rejected |
| Keep only the three entry paths | Preserves thematic choice | Still inserts a second navigation decision after the theory | Rejected |
| Evidence boundary followed by one reading invitation | Short, direct, and conceptually complete | Defers alternate routes and vocabulary | **Chosen** |
