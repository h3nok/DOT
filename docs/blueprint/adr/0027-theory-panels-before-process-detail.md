# ADR-0027: Theory Panels Before Process Detail

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Deciders:** Founder
- **Amends:** ADR-0026 (separate Intent-and-consequence section on the public entry)

## Context

ADR-0026 made the public entry theory-led and established the outside-to-inside
sequence T · E → Big C → RF₀ → Little c. Its first implementation repeated the
hero's concentric circles inside every explanatory section and then introduced a
second, detailed experience-loop diagram immediately afterward.

The repetition made the layer names visually small, while the second diagram
introduced another heading hierarchy just as the reader completed the core
ontology. The landing page needs to help a first reader grasp the concepts, not
display every model the book contains.

## Decision

Each of the four theory sections will be one wide, consistent teaching panel:

- a large typographic identity for T · E, Big C, RF₀, or Little c;
- the layer's epistemic status;
- one explanatory headline and lede;
- parallel “Within the model” and “Evidence boundary” statements; and
- one chapter link.

The panels use the same structure and type roles at every layer. Colour marks
the layer but does not change the information hierarchy.

The detailed experience-loop section is removed from the public entry. The hero
already states the two-way relation between RF₀ and Little c, and the Little c
panel explains that Intent recruits embodied action and encounters consequence.
The full process diagram remains available for a later focused reading surface;
it does not compete with the ontology on the front door.

## Consequences

**Positive.** Layer names become the strongest landmarks after the hero. Wider
measure improves the model/evidence comparison, and one repeated panel grammar
reduces the number of layouts a new reader must decode. Removing the secondary
diagram shortens the conceptual path and serves L1 and L10.

**Negative.** The landing no longer demonstrates every stage of the experience
loop. A reader who wants the process mechanics must continue into Book One or a
focused theory surface.

**Revisit if** readers understand the ontology but consistently fail to discover
how Intent, action, consequence, and Canvas update relate.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Verdict |
| ------ | ---- | ---- | ------- |
| Keep the orbit illustration in every section | Reinforces circular geometry | Repeats the hero and makes the term visually secondary | Rejected |
| Keep the detailed loop directly after Little c | Shows the full process immediately | Adds a second visual grammar before the ontology settles | Rejected |
| Use wide typographic panels and defer process detail | Clear hierarchy; easier comparison; shorter learning path | Less process detail on the front door | **Chosen** |
