## Editorial Verdict

The v4.2 reader-facing manuscript is ready for the author’s line edit and manual refinement.

It does **not** require another 45,000–65,000 words before that work begins. That earlier estimate described one possible path toward a specialist-scale monograph, not a minimum standard for a complete Book One. ## Remaining Author Decisions

These questions no longer make the manuscript internally contradictory because the text labels their uncertainty. They do matter before a future claim of definitive scientific completion.

1. **Is Big C purposive?** If yes, is Love intrinsic to Big C, a policy it adopts, or the human interpretation of non-governance?
2. **How does Little c individuate?** The current maximally unified-stream rule is provisional.
3. **What, if anything, persists beyond biology?** The manuscript separates bare subjectivity, Canvas pattern, Character, and autobiography rather than promising all four.
4. **What is the Intent–biology bridge law?** Rendering Latency remains a defined hypothesis without an independently measurable first endpoint.

The strongest next author question is therefore:

> Is Big C purposive—and if so, what exactly makes its relation to Little c Love rather than indifference, limitation, or non-interference?

## Manual Line-Edit Priorities

During the author pass, work in this order:

1. **Voice.** Replace any sentence that is intellectually correct but does not sound like you.
2. **Lived grounding.** Add only scenes you can tell truthfully and ethically. Two or three precise scenes will do more than twenty pages of abstraction.
3. **Compression.** Where two paragraphs make the same emotional movement, keep the sharper one.
4. **Claim status.** Do not remove qualifiers merely to make the theory sound stronger. Move a claim upward only when its derivation or evidence has changed.
5. **Terminology.** Preserve the distinctions among Canvas, Painting, Character, Little c, Reality Frame, and Reality Stream.
6. **Cadence.** Read the manuscript aloud. The short declarative lines are part of the voice, but they lose force when every transition uses them.
7. **Consent and privacy.** When adding family, migration, work, or spiritual scenes, protect other people’s identifying details unless they have agreed to inclusion.

## Expansion Rule

Add material only when it performs at least one of these functions:

- resolves an explicit author decision;
- supplies a missing bridge premise;
- represents a serious rival more accurately;
- adds evidence necessary for a factual claim;
- gives the reader a lived scene that makes an abstraction observable;
- specifies a measurement, prediction, boundary condition, or revision rule.

If a proposed addition does none of these, it is probably not the next page the book needs.
