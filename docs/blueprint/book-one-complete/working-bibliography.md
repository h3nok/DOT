# Complete Book One — Working Bibliography

This is a research ledger, not the final reference section. Entries are added
when they carry an actual argumentative burden. The final manuscript should
prefer primary papers, canonical formulations by theory proponents, registered
reports, datasets, and direct historical sources over tertiary summaries.

## Source-use notes

- Nagel, Levine, and Chalmers identify different versions of the explanatory
  problem. None independently proves a consciousness-first ontology.
- Block prevents the manuscript from using phenomenal consciousness, access,
  report, and cognitive control as synonyms.
- Frankish supplies a serious rival interpretation of phenomenal-language and
  introspective representation; the manuscript must not dismiss it as simple
  denial.
- Varela and Lutz et al. support disciplined integration of experiential and
  neural data. They do not grant private reports automatic authority.
- Doerig et al., Melloni et al., and the Cogitate Consortium establish the
  comparative and adversarial standard the DOT research program should meet.
- The 2025 Cogitate results challenge important predictions of both IIT and
  GNWT; they do not choose a final metaphysics of consciousness.
