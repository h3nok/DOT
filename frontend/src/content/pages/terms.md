## What is free

Reading Book One online, downloading its PDF, the concept map, the open questions, and the essays are all free. None of them needs an account or a payment. Nothing on this site is sold.

## Free membership

Membership is free and open. Confirm your email with a one-time code to create or open your account. Newsletter subscription is optional and separate. Public reading does not require membership, and membership does not grant access to the owner's private publishing tools or another person's data.

You can sign out, export membership details, or close your membership on the [membership page](/amm/membership). Closing ends member sessions and removes the display name; the account record and email fingerprint remain for verified rejoining. Separately stored work and email subscriptions have their own controls. [Privacy](/privacy) explains the records retained and how to request deletion.

## Optional payments to the author


- You can choose to pay the author directly for the published work and the research behind it. Paying is optional and changes nothing you can read.
- Each payment is one-time, in US dollars, for an amount you choose. Card checkout has a $2 minimum; your bank or payment app sets its own limits for direct transfers. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.
- A payment is not a charitable donation and is not tax-deductible. It buys no goods, access, membership, or standing on the site.
- Available direct-transfer methods are shown on the support page only after the author confirms the recipient. Zelle transfers happen through your banking app; Cash App payments happen through Cash App. Check the recipient and amount before sending. Confirmation comes from your bank or app; this site cannot confirm or cancel a direct transfer.
- Card checkout uses Stripe. Your card details go to Stripe, never to this site, and Stripe emails your receipt. [Privacy](/privacy) lists what the site keeps.

## Refunds

- If you paid by mistake, paid the wrong amount, or changed your mind, write within 30 days of the payment and it will be refunded in full through the original payment method. No reason is needed.
- Include the payment method, date, amount, and transaction reference so the payment can be found. Do not send card numbers, account numbers, or banking credentials.
- For card payments, Stripe returns the money to your card. It usually appears within 5 to 10 business days. Direct-transfer refunds require the author to handle the request through the bank or payment app; this site cannot reverse a transfer.
- For card payments, you can also dispute a charge with your card issuer.
