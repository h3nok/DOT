This glossary fixes the working meanings of DOT’s central terms. It is a semantic contract for Book One, not evidence that the entities named by the model have been established.

## Foundational Architecture

**Big C.** The living, conscious process that, in DOT’s model, emerged within T × E (Speculation), retains state, recognizes patterns including its own, responds to change, and transforms raw input into coherent, structured output. Big C and Little c share the same nature of self-awareness; their scope differs. T and E are prerequisites outside Big C, not capacities contained within it.

**T — primordial continuity—Time.** The continuous flow through which states unfold and change persists. T is an external prerequisite in the T × E account (Speculation), not a capacity contained within Big C. It is not clock time. Measured time belongs to a Reality Frame.

**E — the raw information source. The stuff itself: a source of bits and unformed possibility.** E is an external prerequisite in the T × E account (Speculation), not a capacity contained within Big C. It is not directly experienced; this vocabulary does not establish its intrinsic nature or a literal computational substrate.

**Little c.** The self-aware process at a local scope within Big C. In DOT’s model, Little c is outside RF₀ and is embodied by an Avatar within it, receiving the Frame’s Reality Stream. Its distinctive history and developed identity depend on the state carried by its Canvas. Changes in that state can alter what is available to awareness and action. Book One leaves self-awareness’s intrinsic nature open; localization and body coupling remain mechanisms to be specified.

**Reality Frame (RF).** A rule-bound environment containing a state-space, lawful transitions, constraints, situated interfaces, and consequences. The term can function as a descriptive systems model. DOT adds the stronger ontological hypothesis that Big C generates such Frames.

**RF₀.** DOT’s designation for the physical universe in which this inquiry occurs. Within the model, RF₀ is an experience laboratory for Little cs: their Avatars provide embodied participation in a shared environment of action and consequence. Public evidence establishes a lawful physical universe; it does not independently establish that the universe was generated by Big C.

**Reality Stream (RS).** The time-ordered, embodied experience supplied by a Reality Frame to one Little c through its Avatar. The Stream is situated and selective; it is not a complete copy of the Frame.

**World invariants.** The stable, nonnegotiable constraints of a Reality Frame. In RF₀, the physical sciences progressively formalize these regularities.

**Agency mechanics.** The lawful channels through which embodied action changes a trajectory and consequence returns. Agency mechanics operate within world invariants rather than overriding them.

## Experience, Learning, and Action

**Body or Avatar.** The living biological interface through which Little c receives an RF₀ Reality Stream, acts, and encounters consequence. Avatar does not mean unreal or disposable. The body is adaptive, self-regulating, and indispensable to this life.

**Canvas.** The persistent capacity to carry and update state for a local instance of awareness. Canvas state is inferred through lasting changes in memory, habit, valuation, bodily regulation, relationship, interpretation, and action. DOT’s claim that this capacity is not exhausted by biology remains unverified.

**Painting.** The accumulated organization of Canvas state: expectations, associations, fears, loyalties, meanings, skills, habits, and unresolved contradictions. It shapes interpretation and developed identity; it does not name the underlying self-aware process.

**Character.** The relatively stable action policy expressed when Little c repeatedly interprets and responds through a particular Painting. Character is the Painting made visible through action over time.

**Delta.** A consequential difference that updates the Canvas. A delta is not an exact recording of an event; it is a change in later organization, accessibility, interpretation, or response.

**Intent.** The directional commitment through which Little c selects or stabilizes a move toward action. Intent is distinct from every thought, urge, image, fear, or possible response that appears.

**Pre-Intent draft.** A possible action or interpretation raised by the body, Painting, environment, or present situation before commitment. Drafts may be noticed, revised, rejected, or enacted.

**Experience Loop.** Reality Stream → interpretation through the Painting → pre-Intent drafts → Intent → embodied action → consequence → Canvas update. The loop is a practical model accessible without accepting DOT’s full ontology.

**Rendering Latency (RL).** The hypothesized interval between Intent formation in Little c and the earliest measurable biological change recruited for execution. Current methods do not independently locate the non-neural endpoint required to validate this claim.

**Decision-space.** The meaningful actions that are both lawfully available and visible to the person under present bodily, psychological, relational, and structural conditions.

**Freedom.** Increasing access to truthful and meaningful possibility within constraint. In Book One, effective freedom is graded; it is not omnipotence or absence of cause.

## Developmental and Ethical Terms

**fear.** A lower-case protective signal or state related to predicted threat. This signal can be accurate, disproportionate, learned, or mixed. Its presence is not itself a failure.

**Fear.** The governing contraction that organizes perception and decision-space around predicted threat. Capitalization marks governance, not intensity alone.

**Love.** At the human level, the condition and practice in which Fear no longer governs. Love supports accurate contact, truthful revision, proportionate boundaries, concern for others’ agency, accountability, and repair. At the cosmological level, Love is a further DOT hypothesis about Big C’s non-governing developmental relation to Little c; it is not yet a deduction from consciousness alone.

**Canvas entropy.** DOT’s non-thermodynamic term for the burden of fragmentation within carried state: conflicting policies, divided Intent, attentional capture, unresolved threat, and reduced access to fitting responses. Reducing it means less obstructive conflict, not erasing memory, individuality, or protective sensation.

**Coupling.** Functional connection among Little c, the body or Avatar, the Canvas, and the Reality Frame. It is necessary to embodied experience and learning. Identification or fusion occurs when a person treats a Painting or bodily state as the whole self. The practical terms Body Coupling and Social Coupling describe forms of that identification; they do not make functional connection itself a problem.

**Decoupling Principle.** DOT’s proposal that Little c and the biological interface are tightly coordinated but not ontologically identical. The principle is required by DOT’s architecture; its discriminating empirical signature remains unvalidated. Practical decoupling means noticing a bodily state or learned pattern before automatically identifying with or obeying it. The body and Canvas remain functionally coupled.

**Persistence-under-Entropy Principle (PuE).** The architectural claim that a coherent process persists through continuing detection, correction, adaptation, or maintenance. DOT does not present this term as a new thermodynamic law.

**Limit of Knowledge (LoK).** The constraint that an observer participating through an Avatar in a Reality Frame cannot assume a view from nowhere or claim direct access to the intrinsic nature of the source architecture. LoK restrains certainty; it does not license invention.

## Claim Discipline

**Observation.** An occurrence or measurement that can be stated without already accepting DOT.

**External model.** An account developed in an established field and used within its proper scope. Its inclusion does not imply that its authors endorse DOT.

**DOT derivation.** A claim that follows from DOT’s declared postulates and bridge premises. Internal validity does not establish that the premises are true.

**Speculative extension.** A possibility suggested by the architecture but not adequately derived or tested.

**Epistemic priority.** The claim that evidence used consciously by a subject becomes available to that subject through experience. Epistemic priority does not entail that consciousness is ontologically fundamental.

**Subjective Data Principle.** Feeling and first-person occurrence belong in the evidence base, but their interpretation is fallible. Feeling is data; it is not automatically truth.
